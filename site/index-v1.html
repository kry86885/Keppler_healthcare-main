Detailed module sections

Each module below is a dedicated workspace in HospAI with role-aware access and connected data.

1. Patient Management

Covers registration, OP/IP classification, bed assignment, movement tracking, clinical notes, and visit timeline continuity.

  • Front desk registration and demographic capture
  • Admission, readmission, and ward movement events
  • Medication scheduling and progress note tracking

2. Dashboard & Analytics

Provides management visibility into hospital operations with consolidated indicators from all core modules.

  • IP/OP counts and admission flow trends
  • Accident/surgery and occupancy summaries
  • Revenue and dues view by operational area

3. Billing & Collections

Unifies invoice generation and payment lifecycle for OP/IP/Lab/Pharmacy billing operations.

  • Invoice creation with module-linked charge lines
  • Advance, refund, and settlement workflows
  • Collections, aging, and reconciliation reporting

4. Pharmacy Operations

Manages medicine inventory and dispensing lifecycle with safety and procurement controls.

  • Inventory intake and stock ledger maintenance
  • Low stock, expiry, and damaged stock monitoring
  • Dispensing and pharmacy sales visibility

5. Lab & Diagnostics

Handles diagnostics operations from test intake to billing-linked result lifecycle.

  • Vendor and doctor-wise test management
  • Due/paid status across diagnostic billing
  • Invoice-level diagnostic income reporting

6. HRMS

Supports workforce operations including employee records, attendance, leave, and payroll context.

  • Employee onboarding and department mapping
  • Attendance and leave approval workflows
  • Payroll-support data visibility for operations

Real care environments, diverse workflows

HospAI supports multidisciplinary teams across reception, nursing, diagnostics, pharmacy, finance, and leadership operations.

How daily workflow runs in HospAI

  1. Patient is registered and assigned OP/IP context.
  2. Diagnostics and medication requests flow through connected modules.
  3. Billing records consolidate charges by department.
  4. Payments and dues are tracked with reconciliation support.
  5. Management reviews dashboards and reports for decisions.

Security and governance built into the product

HospAI uses a strict role model (`admin`, `normal`) with module-level access assignment. Employee management remains admin-only. Permissions are backend-enforced and mirrored in frontend navigation to reduce accidental exposure.

  • Default-deny module access for unassigned users.
  • Audit logs for sensitive workflow changes.
  • Export paths for PDF and Excel reporting.
  • Cloud deployment support via Docker Compose/Coolify.
